FAQ

Questions people ask us first.

Is this consulting or implementation?

Both, deliberately. The operating design is only real once it is configured, measured and adopted, so design and implementation are delivered together.

Do you work with our existing sales leadership?

Yes. The operating model is built with the people who run the commercial process, not imposed on them.

How do you measure success?

Against agreed operational indicators: stage conversion, response time, forecast accuracy, adoption and data completeness.
