FAQ

Questions we're asked before this engagement.

Will we lose historical records?

Not by default. We define what must remain queryable in the new system and archive the rest in a structured, retrievable form. Nothing is deleted without written approval.

How is downtime handled?

The legacy system stays live until reconciliation passes. Cutover happens inside an agreed freeze window, usually a weekend, with a tested rollback point.

Can you migrate a CRM another partner built?

Yes, that is a common starting point. We document what exists, keep what supports the operating model, and rebuild the rest.
