# Privacy policy.
This policy explains what happens to the information you submit through opsmo.tech.

## 
@legal
1. What we collect :: When you submit a contact form or book a call we collect the details you provide: name, business email, company, website, country, phone, industry, company size, current systems, service interest, the operating challenge you describe, user count, timeline, optional budget and any additional requirements. We also record the page you submitted from so we understand which content is useful.
1. Why we collect it :: Only to respond to your enquiry, assess whether and how we can help, prepare a scoped recommendation, and keep a record of the commercial conversation. We do not sell data, and we do not add you to a marketing list without a separate, explicit opt-in.
1. How it is stored :: Submissions are stored in the application's secured database and are visible only to authorised OPSMO administrators. Access is governed by role-based permissions. Where a CRM synchronisation is configured, the same record may be transmitted to our CRM over an authenticated server-side connection; until that integration is enabled, the record remains in this application and is flagged as pending synchronisation.
1. Email :: You receive an acknowledgment when you submit a form, and our team is notified internally. Transactional email is sent through our platform provider. Booking requests are confirmed by email once a time is agreed.
1. Cookies and tracking :: This website does not set advertising or third-party analytics cookies. Session and authentication cookies are used only where you sign in to a secured area, and are required for that session to function.
1. Retention :: Enquiry records are retained while a commercial conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for reference. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.
1. Your rights :: You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted. Send the request through the contact form and we will action it within a reasonable period.
1. Changes :: If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page. This policy describes website handling only; client engagements are governed by their signed agreement.
