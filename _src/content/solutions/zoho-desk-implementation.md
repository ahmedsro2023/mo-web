## What we set up.
@cards3
* 01 · Ticket intake and routing :: Bring requests from email, forms and your other support channels into Zoho Desk and assign them by team, issue type or priority.
* 02 · Response targets and escalations :: Set response and resolution targets, with reminders and escalation rules when a ticket needs attention.
* 03 · Help centre and knowledge base :: Publish answers to common questions and give customers one place to submit and track their requests.
* 04 · Zoho CRM integration :: Give agents relevant customer history while they work, and let sales see open support issues on the account.
* 05 · Support dashboards :: Track backlog, first response time, resolution time and recurring issues in views your team actually reviews.
* 06 · Team training :: Train agents and supervisors on the daily workflow, the escalation rules and how to keep ticket data clean.
