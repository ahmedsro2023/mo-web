^ BOUNDARIES
## Maintenance is included. Development is quoted.
We don't sell unlimited development. A retainer protects and improves the system you have; new capability is a separate, scoped piece of work.
@cols2
### INCLUDED IN A RETAINER
- Administration, user and permission changes
- Fixes to existing workflows and automations
- Configuration adjustments within the current architecture
- Dashboard and report updates
- Monitoring, troubleshooting and incident response
### QUOTED SEPARATELY
- New applications, modules or portals
- New integrations and data sources
- Large data migrations
- Re-architecture of the current data model
- Projects with their own delivery timeline

^ HOW IT WORKS
## From assessment to a managed service.
1. Assessment :: We review your current system, support history and change backlog to size the right tier.
1. Agreement :: Scope, included hours, response targets and exclusions are written down before work starts.
1. Operations :: Requests are routed through a single queue with tracked status, prioritisation and reporting.
1. Review :: A monthly review covers system health, adoption and the improvement plan; the tier is revisited at renewal.
