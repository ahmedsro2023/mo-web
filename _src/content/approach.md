^ OPERATING PRINCIPLES
## Four commitments that shape every engagement.
@cards4
* Business first :: No configuration begins before the operating process is understood and agreed. Software is the last decision, not the first.
* Architecture over configuration :: A system that grows with the business needs a data model, not a pile of workarounds. We document what we build.
* Adoption is the deliverable :: A technically perfect system nobody uses has failed. Training, enablement and governance are part of the scope.
* One accountable owner :: You deal with the person who designed the system, not a rotating queue of tickets.
