^ HOW WE THINK
## Four rules we follow on every project.
@cards4
* Business first :: We don't configure anything until we understand how your team actually works today. The software is the last decision, not the first.
* Built to last :: We set up a clean structure that can grow with you, and we document everything we build.
* People must use it :: A perfect system nobody uses has failed. Training and support are part of the project, not extras.
* One owner :: You work with the person who designed your system, not a different support agent every week.

^ PACKAGES
## Three ways to start.
We don't publish fixed prices, because every business is different. We agree the scope, timeline and price in writing before any work begins.
$ [PACKAGE 1] CRM Foundation :: For teams that need a clean, working CRM. :: Discovery of your sales process; CRM setup and data structure; Pipeline, roles and permissions; Lead capture and assignment rules; Core reports and dashboards; Data import and duplicate clean-up; Team training and handover :: :: A working sales system: clean data, a clear pipeline and reports your managers can trust.
$ [PACKAGE 2] Connected Business :: For teams that need CRM, automation, reports and integrations. :: Everything in CRM Foundation; Workflows and approvals; Website and channel integration; Finance or ERP connection; Alerts when an automation fails; Management reports; Support after go-live :: :: Your CRM connected to finance, marketing and your website, with automations and reports that run on their own.
$ [PACKAGE 3] Enterprise Transformation :: For larger, multi-department or multi-company projects. :: Everything in Connected Business; Multi-company structure and governance; Integration across several platforms; Large data migration; Custom modules and portals; Executive dashboards and KPIs; Phased rollout and change management :: :: A phased programme across departments and systems, with migration and executive reporting.
