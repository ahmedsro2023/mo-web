^ MONTHLY RETAINERS
## Three levels of ongoing support.
Your CRM keeps changing after launch. A retainer keeps it healthy and improving, with clear limits agreed in writing.
$ [RETAINER 1] Essential · CRM Care :: For a stable, well-run system. :: CRM administration; Adding users and managing access; Fixing issues; Small setting changes; Monthly system review; Support requests :: New modules or apps · New integrations · Large data projects
$ [RETAINER 2] Growth · Ongoing Improvement :: For teams that want the system to keep getting better. :: Everything in Essential; Improving workflows; Maintaining and repairing automations; Better reports; Watching integrations; Refresher training; Monthly improvement plan :: New apps · Large integrations · Cross-department programmes
$ [RETAINER 3] Enterprise · Managed Transformation :: For businesses that want ongoing technical leadership. :: Everything in Growth; Integration oversight; Operations across several platforms; Solution architecture advice; Executive reporting; Improvement roadmap; Governance :: Unlimited development hours · Third-party licence costs · Work outside the agreed roadmap
! Fees, included hours, response times and renewal terms are agreed per business and confirmed in writing. Nothing is charged until the scope is signed.

^ WHAT'S INCLUDED
## Maintenance is included. New development is quoted.
A retainer protects and improves the system you already have. New features are a separate, scoped project.
@cols2
### INCLUDED IN A RETAINER
- Adding users and changing permissions
- Fixing existing workflows and automations
- Setting changes inside your current setup
- Updating dashboards and reports
- Monitoring, troubleshooting and fixing incidents
### QUOTED SEPARATELY
- New apps, modules or portals
- New integrations and data sources
- Large data migrations
- Rebuilding the data structure
- Projects with their own timeline

^ HOW IT STARTS
## From first review to monthly support.
1. Review :: We look at your current system, past support requests and what you want changed, then recommend a level.
1. Agreement :: Scope, included hours, response times and exclusions are written down before work starts.
1. Support :: Requests go into one queue, with clear status, priorities and reporting.
1. Monthly review :: We review system health, usage and the improvement plan every month.
