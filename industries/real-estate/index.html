The problem

Why real-estate teams lose good leads.

It is rarely the agents. It is the missing system behind them.

  1. 01

    Leads come from everywhere

    Property Finder, Bayut, your website, Meta ads and WhatsApp all send enquiries. Without one inbox, some are answered twice and some never.

  2. 02

    Speed decides the deal

    The agent who calls back first usually wins the client. Manual assignment means minutes become hours.

  3. 03

    Agents keep their own lists

    When follow-ups live in personal phones and spreadsheets, the company loses the history when an agent leaves.

  4. 04

    Managers can't see the pipeline

    Which source brings real buyers? Which agent converts? Without clean data, nobody knows.

      FAQ

      Questions real-estate teams ask us.

      Can Zoho receive Property Finder and Bayut leads automatically?

      Yes, where the portal gives API or email-lead access for your account. Leads land in Zoho CRM with the listing, source and campaign attached, and are assigned to an agent straight away.

      We already have a CRM. Do we have to start over?

      No. We review what you have first. If it can do the job, we fix it. If it can't, we move your contacts, deals and history to Zoho and check them record by record.

      How long does a real-estate setup take?

      A standard brokerage setup usually takes four to six weeks, including testing and team training. Larger developers with several projects are rolled out in phases.

      Can the team work in Arabic and English?

      Yes. Zoho supports both, and we train teams in Arabic and English.

      Third-party integrations are built only where the provider's API or documented interface allows it. No partnership with any portal, marketplace or channel is implied.
