SALESFORCE

Moving from Salesforce, or fixing it.

Many teams pay for Salesforce and only use a small part of it. We help in two ways. If Salesforce still fits, we clean up the setup so reports and automations can be trusted again. If it no longer fits, we move you to Zoho One with your history, owners and reports intact. PropSpace made this move: it left eight separate tools, including Salesforce, for one Zoho One system.

Best for

Sales teams with complex processes, several teams, or a Salesforce setup that has grown messy over the years.
