THE STORY

A decade inside commercial teams, sales operations and technology, and one conclusion that shaped OPSMO.

I've spent my career working inside businesses, not just advising them from the outside.

From Meeting Minds and Property Finder to Zoho, Motivate Media Group, PropSpace, and other ventures across the UAE, I've worked across sales, business development, commercial operations, CRM, technology, and business transformation.

As Zoho's first Egyptian hire and the first team member in Zoho Egypt, I had the opportunity to help develop a market, build the team, work directly with businesses, and understand something that shaped the way I approach technology today:

Most businesses don't have a software problem. They have an operational problem.

I've seen companies invest in powerful systems while their teams continue working through spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, disconnected departments, and manual processes.

I've seen sales teams generate opportunities that operations couldn't track. Management teams make decisions without reliable data. And businesses purchase more applications when what they really needed was to connect the ones they already had.

The problem was rarely a lack of technology.

It was the way the business operated.

After more than a decade working across industries, from real estate and technology to media, events, and professional services, one thing became clear:

Digital transformation doesn't begin with software. It begins with understanding how a business actually works.

That's why I built OPSMO.

To bring commercial understanding and technical execution together. To design, implement, integrate, and continuously improve the systems businesses depend on.

Not simply to deliver a CRM and walk away.

But to make sure the technology works, the people use it, and the business operates better because of it.

Technology is the infrastructure. Business performance is the outcome.
